Happy Birthday ❤️

Dear Brit Knee,

Happy birthday, sweetheart. While making this gift for you, I reminisced on the memories of us. You're someone that's made me want to pull my hair out but also eat you up, all at once. But after all, I write this with love. As you know, I have an oddly singular focus when it comes to you. A sort of obsessive desire to see you succeed. I truly hope you blossom into the dewiest, most colorful flower you are. It'd be a shame to the world if you stayed in your bud. In support of this dream, I wanted to make you something fun and cute that could be a constant reminder and motivator to keep you on track, and to bring you luck.

Along with your monster pet "bean" that you can bring around with you for GREAT luck, I've created a new quest to help you keep on top of your goals. I hope it's useful. If not... that's okay too. Either way, all of the small things will pay off for you, I know it. I hope that you know what I'm talking about by now. As the famous quote goes, "Luck is when preparation meets opportunity." When the right opportunity comes to you, I hope that you'll be prepared to seize it. So ultimately, this is a gift that I hope you'll dispose of eventually. If there's any soppy words, I may or may not tell them to you in-person. But I will write that I wish we could have hugged more. And all the other stuff too. Maybe I would have been less mean. Regardless, as of now, I love you and wish you a very happy birthday.

I hope this year brings you nothing but happiness, peace, and so many moments that make you smile.

- Kyle
